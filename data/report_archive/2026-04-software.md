# Lather Log - April 2026

**Total Shaves:** 1,333
**Unique Shavers:** 92

Welcome to your SOTD Lather Log for April 2026

* 1,333 shave reports from 92 distinct shavers during the month of April 2026 were analyzed to produce this report. Collectively, these shavers used 512 distinct soaps from 128 distinct brands. 14 of those shaves used samples of 12 unique soaps, and 1 used a mashup.

## Observations

* After its brief March Madness-fueled tour of the rarified air of the podium, Fanzine Saponifications falls from 3rd to back 25th in this more typical month as the Soap Brand podium reverts to standard programming of Barrister and Mann, Stirling, and Catie’s Bubbles.

* u/BossHoggins10's brain bowl mission pushed MdC Fougère to the top of the Soaps table and him to the top of the Most Boring Shaver table with a perfect 100% HHI score.

* At the opposite end of the spectrum u/brokenjaw622's continued dedication to Catie's Bubbles helped push the brand to the top of the Brand Diversity table. And his 30 shaves with 30 different CB scents pushed him to the top of both the Soap Diversity by User table and the Exclusive Soaps table, a new contest that rewards a shaver for being the only user of a soap in a month.

* Crowne & Crane, Back to Basics Log Cabin, Bartigan & Stark, Clubman Pinaud, Aion Skincare, and Tcheon Fung Sing all reached the Brand table despite being driven entirely by a single dedicated user.

* In other news, April’s data aligned into perfect programmer math: 128 brands (2⁷) and 512 soaps (2⁹) give the month a curiously binary-looking inventory distribution.

## Notes & Caveats

* Tables show all entries with 5 or more shaves to provide comprehensive coverage without artificial row limits.

* The unique user column shows the number of different users who used a given brand/soap/etc in the month.

* The Brand Diversity table details the number of distinct soaps used during the month from that particular brand, showing all brands with 5+ unique soaps.

* The change Δ vs columns show how an item has moved up or down the rankings since that month. = means no change in position, up or down arrows indicate how many positions up or down the rankings an item has moved compared to that month. n/a means the item was not present in that month.

* When available, soap names in the Soaps table link to their entry on Wet Shaving Database, where you can see scent notes, descriptions, and ingredient lists.

* An **exclusive soap** is a matched brand+scent used by only one shaver in the month. That shaver earns one point for that soap, no matter how many times they logged it. Hat tip to the members of the r/wetshaving discord for suggesting the idea.

## Most Boring Shaver
Boring Score is the chance that two shaves picked at random from a user used the same soap (higher means more repetitive). Effective Soaps converts that into an equivalent even-rotation count: the number of soaps they would need to use equally often to produce the same score. Example: someone can log 20 soaps but still have ~2 Effective Soaps if one soap dominates roughly 70% of their shaves and the rest are just occasional. In the case of tied HHI, Shaves descending is the tie breaker, since it's clearly more boring to use the same soap for 20 shaves than it is for 10.

For the mathematically inclined: Boring Score is a diversity/concentration index (HHI in economics, Simpson’s index in ecology). More detail at [Wikipedia: Diversity index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diversity_index).
